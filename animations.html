See the techniques work

Short animations of the methods biomedical science runs on: how each sequencing platform reads DNA, what happens in a PCR tube cycle by cycle, how each kind of microscope makes its image, how reporter genes report, how CRISPR-Cas9 edits, and how a serial dilution becomes a standard curve.

Each one plays in labelled stages. Pause on any stage, step back and forth, or tap a numbered stage to jump to it. With reduced motion switched on in your system settings, each stage is shown finished instead of playing. The same animations are in the app.

To send these to someone, download all of them as one file. It works offline, opens in any web browser, and can be attached to an email.

The animations need JavaScript.
