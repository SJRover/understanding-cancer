BioMed Companion

See the techniques work

22 short animations of the methods biomedical science runs on: sequencing, PCR and qPCR, microscopes, reporter genes, CRISPR-Cas9 and serial dilution.

Pick one from the list. Each plays in labelled stages and pauses at the end of every stage long enough to read the explanation; the bar under the picture shows how far through the stage you are. Pause at any point, step back and forth, or tap a numbered stage to jump straight to it.

Everything is inside this one file, so it works without an internet connection and can be passed on as it is.
