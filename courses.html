What is free, and what is not

What Is Cancer is published complete, and it is free in the app too, so the free tier is the same thing in both places.

The other seven publish their opening third and then stop. That is enough to judge whether the material is any good, which is the only thing a preview is honestly for.

None of them include the revision. There is no spaced repetition, no multiple choice and no progress tracking on this site. That is the whole difference: the web is for reading, the app is for remembering.

Why the sources are on the first slide

Because you should not have to take any of this on trust. Each course opens with the papers it was built from, linked, and every one of them is open access, so you can read the original rather than a description of it. Where a slide states standard textbook material it says so, and where a conclusion is the course's own it says that too.
