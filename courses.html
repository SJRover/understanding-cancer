What is on this site, and what is in the app

What Is Cancer is published complete here. The other seven publish their opening third and then stop, which is enough to judge whether the material is any good.

All eight are complete in the app, and the app is free: there is no purchase and no subscription. The app is also where the revision is: spaced repetition, exam-style questions with explanations, and progress tracking. The web is for reading, the app is for remembering.

Why the sources are on the first slide

Because you should not have to take any of this on trust. Each course opens with the papers it was built from, linked, and every one of them is open access, so you can read the original rather than a description of it. Where a slide states standard textbook material it says so, and where a conclusion is the course's own it says that too.
