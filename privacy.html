Privacy policy

BioMed Companion does not collect anything about you. There is no account to make, nothing to sign in to, and no analytics of any kind in the app.

The short version: your progress lives on your phone and is never sent anywhere. The only time the app uses the network at all is when you search for papers, or tap a link to open one.

What is stored, and where

Which cards you have answered, when they are next due, your practice answers, your streak and your settings are saved on your device using Apple's own storage for apps. That data never leaves the phone. It is not sent to me, it is not backed up to any server of mine, and I have no way to read it.

Deleting the app deletes it.

When the app uses the network

Searching for papers

The paper search, under Learn, searches Europe PMC, which is run by EMBL's European Bioinformatics Institute. When you search, the words you typed are sent to Europe PMC so it can return results. Nothing is attached to that request that identifies you, and the app does not keep a record of what you searched for. Europe PMC has its own privacy notice, which governs what it does with the request.

Opening a paper or a link

Tapping a paper or one of the careers links opens that page in a browser inside the app. Those sites are not mine and their own privacy policies apply once you are there.

That is the whole list

The app makes no other network requests. The courses, the diagrams, the questions, the glossary and the careers section are all inside the app and work with no connection at all.

No purchases

The app is free and has no in-app purchases, so no payment information is involved at any point.

The Courses tab and its access code

If you type an access code to open Courses, the app checks it on your device and keeps a key derived from it, so the tab stays open next time. The code is not stored and neither the code nor the key is sent anywhere. Locking the tab again deletes the key. Bookmarks and your place in a course are kept on your device as positions only.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, it is scheduled on your device by iOS and its text is worked out on your device. Nothing is sent through a notification server, and turning it off in Settings stops it.

Other companies

There are no advertising networks, no analytics services, no crash reporting services and no social media components in the app. It contains no third party tracking code of any kind. The only outside service it ever contacts is Europe PMC, and only when you search.

Children

The app is written for adult and older student readers, and since it collects no data at all it collects none from children either.

Your rights

Data protection law gives you rights to see, correct and delete data held about you. Since none is held about you, there is nothing for me to show or delete. The data on your own device is yours, and removing the app removes it.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be on this page with a new date at the bottom. If a future version of the app ever collected anything, that change would be described here before it shipped.
