Support

If something is not working, or a fact in a course looks wrong, write to sjrovers1995@gmail.com and I will answer.

It helps to say which iPhone you have, which version of iOS it is running, and which course and slide you were on. A screenshot is usually faster than a description.

Common questions

I bought the courses and they are locked

Open the Progress tab, scroll to the bottom, and tap Restore purchases. That asks Apple whether the Apple Account signed in on this device owns the unlock. If it says nothing was found, check that the device is signed in to the same Apple Account you bought with, in the iOS Settings app under your name, then try again.

What does the purchase include

What Is Cancer is free and always will be. One purchase of £7.99 unlocks the other seven courses permanently on every device signed in to your Apple Account. It is not a subscription and there is nothing to cancel.

Can I get a refund

Refunds are handled by Apple rather than by me. Go to reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and choose the purchase.

Does it work without a connection

Yes, apart from searching for papers and opening a paper, which both need one. The courses, questions, diagrams, glossary and careers section are all inside the app.

Where is my progress kept, and can I move it

On your device. There is no account, so there is nothing to sign in to on a second phone, and progress does not follow you between devices. Deleting the app deletes the progress with it.

A diagram is too small to read

Tap any diagram to open it, then pinch to zoom. Turning the phone sideways gives them more room too.

Can I turn the sounds off

Yes. Progress, then Settings, where sound and haptics have their own switches.

I think a fact is wrong

Please tell me, and say which slide. Every course is written from open access papers and each claim is checked against the source text, but that is a process run by a person and people make mistakes. Corrections are welcome and I would rather fix one than leave it.

Where does the content come from

Open access research papers, all under licences that permit reuse. Each course names its sources on its first slide and links straight to them, so you can read the original rather than take the course's word for it.

Accessibility

The app supports Dynamic Type, works in portrait and landscape, and every diagram can be pinched to zoom. If something is hard to use with VoiceOver or at a larger text size, that is worth an email: it is the kind of problem that is easy to fix once someone reports it.
